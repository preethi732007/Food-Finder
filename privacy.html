Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account or submit inquiries through our contact forms, we collect information such as your name, email address, user role (Donor or Donee), and message content to manage your account and facilitate food coordination.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to provide and maintain the FoodBridge service, communicate updates regarding food pickups, authenticate your login session, and respond to support inquiries.

3. Data Security

We implement secure database storage practices using Supabase to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. Information is only shared when necessary to coordinate safe food donations between verified community members.
